# R5: the regulator's sound (KeysAudio)

Nothing has to change in the hub for the regulator to be heard: the diver plays it through the audio actor's public
one-shot synth (`AKeysAudio::Burst`, in `AKeysDiver::BreathSound`, `KeysDiver.cpp`), which already runs everything
under the underwater low-pass. Nothing is bought and no sample is used. This note says what is synthesised, so the
marshal can move it into `KeysAudio` if it should live there, and lists the one hub change that would make it better.

## What plays now (from the diver's file)

Each breath is one cycle of `KeysScuba::FState::BreathPh`: 5.2 s at rest down to 2.6 s sprinting. In for the first
38%, a pause, out from 45% to 90%.

- Breath in (event `BreathIn`):
  - the demand valve's click: band-passed noise at 1,500 Hz, Q 5, gain 0.05, attack 2 ms, 35 ms long;
  - the hiss: band-passed noise sweeping 620 -> 980 Hz, Q 1.4, gain 0.085 (0.125 sprinting), attack 35% of its
    length, as long as the breath in (1.0 to 2.0 s).
- Breath out (event `BreathOut`):
  - a rumble: low-passed noise sweeping 260 -> 150 Hz, gain 0.10 (0.15 sprinting), attack 60 ms, as long as the
    breath out (1.2 to 2.3 s);
  - bubble pops over it: 9 (17 sprinting) band-passed noise bursts, each 280-900 Hz rising by half, Q 6, gain
    0.05-0.10 falling to 40% by the end of the breath, 50-110 ms long, spread over 90% of the breath out.
- Nothing plays with the camera out of the water, with the gas gone, or blacked out.

The diver also passes `bHolding = false` to `SetUnderwater` while she is breathing from the cylinder, so the
breath-hold heartbeat and the louder held-breath loop stay off until the gas is gone; then they come back as on a
free-dive.

## The hub change that would improve it (optional, not needed for merge)

`AKeysAudio::SetUnderwater` keeps its quiet idle breathing loop (`Loops.BreathGain` 0.04) and the mask's bubble pops
(every 1.5 to 4 s) running when `bHolding` is false. On scuba those sit under the regulator and slightly blur the
silence between breaths. A fifth argument `bool bRegulator` that zeroes `Loops.BreathGain` / `BreathDepth` and skips
the `BubbleT` pops would clean that up; the diver would pass `Sc.OnGas()`.

`AKeysDiver::OnBreath` (a `TFunction<void(bool bInhale, double Depth, double Effort)>`) is there for the hub to hook
if the sound moves into `KeysAudio`; remove `BreathSound` from the diver then, or both will play.

## Not verified

The sound was not listened to: a scripted run leaves screenshots and a log, not audio. The log shows no audio
errors with it running. Levels were chosen against the existing bursts in `KeysAudio.cpp` (bubble pops 0.04-0.09,
heartbeat 0.16-0.22) and need an ear.
