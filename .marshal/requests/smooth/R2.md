# R2: the "pixelated" water at the trolling strike (Content/Python/init_unreal.py, OCEAN_FOAM; M_KR_Ocean regenerated)

Cause (found, not fixable in the lane's files): the white-water patch every big splash leaves
(`AKeysLife::Splash` -> `KeysGeo::EmitTrail`, aeration Tr.Y -> UV0.y "Aer") goes through the wash-streak warp in
OCEAN_FOAM:

    float2 tp = float2(dot(p, td) * 0.3, dot(p, float2(-td.y, td.x)));
    p = lerp(p, tp, saturate(Aer * 1.6));

`p` is the absolute world position in metres (thousands), so wherever Aer varies across the patch the noise
coordinate moves by thousands of cells per metre: the value noise and the lace cells alias into nested squares and
stair-step lines (evidence/c3_crop_squares_strkD64.png, c3_crop_squares_strkE65.png). Seen from the raise/strike
shots a few metres off, the patch fills the frame. Proof: the same run with the splash's EmitTrail switched off
locally (`-KRNoSplashWash`, a test edit of KeysLife.cpp, reverted, never committed) shows no squares at all
(evidence/c3_strike_no_splash_wash_test_strkG.png). The boat's own wash may show the same fold at its edges.

Wanted (marshal's choice):
1. In OCEAN_FOAM, blend the two noise results instead of the coordinates: evaluate the noise and lace once at `p`
   where k = saturate(Aer*1.6) is 0, once at `tp` where it is 1, and both only in between (`lerp(n(p), n(tp), k)`);
   or make the streak frame local (subtract a per-patch origin) so |tp - p| stays small.
2. Cheaper stop-gap: splashes (`AKeysLife::Splash`) leave foam with no aeration (A0 = A1 = 0), so they skip the
   streak warp; the boat's wash keeps it.
Then re-shoot: `RX=2560 RY=1440 shoot.sh strk "<mk5 script, strikefix=1>"` at gfx=1 and gfx=2, crops of the fish
at the splash before/after.
